# Team Collaboration E-Book Platform 📖

> A web-based solution developed to solve resource scarcity during a collaborative academic project.

---

### 📝 Background & Motivation
This project was born out of a practical need during a **National Defense Studies** course. Our team was tasked with a comprehensive final report based on a specific textbook. However, only **one physical copy** of the textbook was available for the entire group. 

To enable efficient parallel workflow and ensure every member had access to the material simultaneously, I digitized the essential content and built this localized E-book platform.

### 💡 The Problem
* **Resource Bottleneck**: Group members had to wait for their turn to read the single physical book, causing a significant delay in the research phase.
* **Collaboration Barrier**: Difficulty in cross-referencing and discussing specific chapters in real-time.

### 🛠️ The Solution
I developed a lightweight, mobile-responsive web reader and deployed it via **GitHub Pages**. 
* **Accessibility**: Converted physical text into a searchable, digital format accessible from any device.
* **Efficiency**: Reduced the team's research time by an estimated 60% by enabling concurrent reading and note-taking.
* **Fast Deployment**: Utilized GitHub Pages for instant hosting with zero cost.

### 🔧 Tech Stack
* **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
* **Hosting**: GitHub Pages
* **Design Focus**: Readability, mobile responsiveness, and fast loading speeds.

---

### 📈 Impact & Results
* **Empowered the Team**: Successfully supported a team of 5+ members to complete the report ahead of schedule.
* **Demonstrated Leadership**: Showcased the ability to identify operational bottlenecks and implement technical solutions to improve team productivity.
